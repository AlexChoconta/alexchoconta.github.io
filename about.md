# About


I am a Colombian anthropologist and feminist researcher currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Gender, Women, and Sexuality Studies at the University of Minnesota. I also hold a Master’s degree in Gender Studies at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia. My work moves across feminist theory, Latin American cultural studies, visual culture, and digital media studies, with a particular interest in how power, affect, and everyday life are negotiated through contemporary discourses of empowerment and development.

Since 2013, I have worked alongside urban, rural, and Indigenous young women in Colombia through research, community-based projects, and feminist organizing. 

My research explores how contemporary discourses of empowerment, entrepreneurship, and peacebuilding shape young Colombian women’s everyday lives across digital platforms, public policy, and popular economies. Bringing together feminist theory, visual culture, affect studies, and ethnographic research in Colombia, I examine how gendered aspirations, infrastructures, and forms of survival emerge within landscapes marked by extraction, inequality, and the unresolved afterlives of violence.

My work has been supported by the Interdisciplinary Doctoral Fellowship (2023–2024), the College of Liberal Arts Doctoral Dissertation Fellowship (2025), and the IAS Research and Creative Collaboratives Fellowship (2026 - 2027).
