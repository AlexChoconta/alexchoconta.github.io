# About


I am a Colombian anthropologist and feminist researcher currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Gender, Women, and Sexuality Studies at the University of Minnesota. I also hold a Master’s degree in Gender Studies at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia. My work moves across feminist theory, Latin American cultural studies, visual culture, and digital media studies, with a particular interest in how power, affect, and everyday life are negotiated through contemporary discourses of empowerment and development.

Since 2013, I have worked alongside urban, rural, and Indigenous young women in Colombia through research, community-based projects, and feminist organizing. My doctoral dissertation examines how young Colombian women navigate the discourse of “empowerment girlhood” through entrepreneurship, peacebuilding initiatives, social media platforms, public policy, and commercial spaces such as trade fairs. 

More broadly, I am interested in how infrastructures, media, and capitalist imaginaries shape contemporary forms of gendered subjectivity and social belonging.

My work has been supported by the Interdisciplinary Doctoral Fellowship (2023–2024), the CLA DDF Fall 2025 Award, and the IAS Research and Creative Collaboratives Fellowship (2026 - 2027).
